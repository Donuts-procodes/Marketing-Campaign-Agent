# Campaigning Strategy Agent

---
This AI Marketing Text Generator requires three core inputs:

1. **campaign objective description**
2. **target audience characteristics**
3. **preferred brand tone**

---

Campaign Assistant is an AI content marketing tool that generates three essential marketing asset categories:

1. **Landing page copy** with conversion-focused headline, content, and CTA elements
2. **Email marketing campaigns** including subject lines and engagement-focused body content
3. **Advertising copy** that is tailored for Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and LinkedIn ads
